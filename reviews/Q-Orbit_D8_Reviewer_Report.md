# D8 REVIEWER 1 — QUANTUM CRYPTOGRAPHY THEORIST

## 1. Overall assessment

This is an unusually disciplined theoretical manuscript. Every headline quantity I could check against the controlled record reproduces **bit-exactly in binary64** (margin identity Eq. (3), QBER identity, screen fraction, the 256.5669430839006-bit penalty at (ε_s, ε_c)=(10⁻¹⁰, 10⁻⁹)); the "21" decomposition identity including the +1 chain-rule constants is **algebraically exact**, and the claim that omitting them leaves the right-hand side exactly 2 bits short is confirmed numerically (difference −2.0). The finite-key derivation is a faithful transcription of the Lim et al. (2014) / Sidhu et al. (2022) F4+F3 construction; ε_char is defined exactly once (§9.4, Eq. (5)) with the double-counting prohibition stated explicitly; the ideal f_EC=1 leakage accounting is disclosed and correctly propagated to the "all margins are upper bounds w.r.t. error-correction efficiency" qualification everywhere the margin is quoted; and the candidate-key convention K=⌊max(M,0)⌋ is stated and consistently applied. I found no point where the manuscript overstates its security coverage: every proof-coverage gap is labelled, the two BLOCKING rows and two open proof problems are honestly declared, and Profile B's preprint anchor is correctly downgraded per the CFR L-09 / red-team C1 and F-03 record. No hidden implementation assumptions were found inside the QBER/loss/efficiency scalars — the scalar detection model is printed in §8 and each of its blind spots is mapped in §12/Table 2. My findings are precision/wording issues and evidence gaps, not errors.

## 2. Major comments

1. **§9.3 "21" decomposition — verified exact (strength).** Under the symmetric split α₂=α₃=ν̄=ε_s/21, I computed [2log₂(1/α₂)+1]+[2log₂(1/α₃)+1]+2log₂(1/(2ν̄)) = 6·log₂(21/ε_s) with binary64 difference 0.0; without the +1 chain-rule constants the result is short by exactly 2.0 bits, matching the manuscript's claim and the D3 §6.1 / bridge §2.3 record. The constituent count also checks: 2(2α₁+α₂+α₃)+ν̄+10ε₁+2ε₂ = 8+1+10+2 = 21 under the split. This is the correct Lim et al. supplementary Eqs. (13)–(14) structure, and the faithfulness condition (all fluctuation sub-terms at effective deviation ε_s/21; the fixture's β=ln(21/ε_s)) is correctly stated and correctly flagged as model-inspection evidence.

2. **Margin identity, penalty, and floor convention — bit-exact (strength).** Recomputing Eq. (3) from the Table 3 locked values (s_X,0=5,047.784882329125; s_X,1=183,803.04893680647; φ_X=0.09270161340569935; λ_EC=65,385.40180119235; penalty=256.5669430839006) yields M=41,338.62418456675 with difference 0.0, and m_X/n_X=0.017422686665352745 with difference 0.0. The candidate-key convention K=⌊max(M,0)⌋ is the correct conservative fixed-length convention and the signed-margin retention is sound methodology.

3. **Phase-error treatment Eq. (2) is correct and conservative, but the cap's justification is only implicit (weakness in exposition, not in math).** φ_X=min(v_Z,1/s_Z,1+γ, ½) uses upper/lower bounds for v_Z,1/s_Z,1 (correctly composed inside the 21) and the cap at ½ is exactly the worst-case choice: h₂ is maximized at ½, so for any true phase error e≤φ with φ>½ the adversarial worst case is h₂=1, zeroing the single-photon term — which is also the manuscript's stated mechanism for the 1,088/1,681 cap-binding rows. The manuscript should state this one-line justification, since a reader could otherwise suspect min(·,½) *understates* key by discarding the h₂-symmetry region (it does not; e_ph>½ gives no valid positive-rate inference in this proof family).

4. **Upper-envelope logic of the screen — correct (strength).** The claim "the positive count at any fixed window is at most 568" follows because per-point argmax gives M(p)≥margin_w(p) for every fixed w, so positives under any fixed w are a subset of the 568. The non-probabilistic reading is stated in §11.3, §15, and the claim ledger (C13, CL-55) without contradiction anywhere.

5. **f_EC=1 disclosure — honest and correctly directed (strength).** λ_EC=65,385.4 bits = 1.047×n_X·h₂(QBER_X) (62,447.1), consistent with "ideal f_EC=1 plus a finite-size quantile correction"; since realistic f_EC>1 strictly increases leakage, "upper bounds with respect to error-correction efficiency" is the correct direction, and it is carried in §9.3, §11.1, §14.1 (rule 2, "no hardcoded f_EC"), §15, §16 item 4, and claims C8/C9. The f_EC≈1.16 figure is quarantined as literature context only.

6. **ε accounting — composably correct, no double counting (strength).** ε_char≡Σ_jδ_j appears as a definition once (Eq. (5)); §14.1 and Table 8 use δ_j only as per-parameter failure probabilities feeding that single union-bound entry; Eq. (6) is the correct joint bound Pr[(insecure∨incorrect)∧approved] ≤ ε_c+ε_s+ε_char(+ε_auth) per the Tan–Nahar framework, and the conditional-on-approval prohibition (§14.3) matches the bridge document §1.2/§1.5. The non-unique recovery of (10⁻¹⁰,10⁻⁹) from the single penalty constraint is handled fail-closed (EV-9), which is the right call.

7. **Profile A / Profile B positioning — consistent with the controlled record (strength).** Layer 1 (hardened Lim/Sidhu + assumption ledger + Kato/Mannalath statistics, CONDITIONAL-COMPUTATION-labelled) matches D4 §4 recommendation 1; Layer 2's anchor (Tupkary et al. arXiv:2601.18035) is labelled PREPRINT with its own future-work framing quoted and imperfection coverage correctly restated as resting on Currás-Lorenzo et al., Optica Quantum 3, 525 (2025). ## 3. Minor comments

1. **§13, Layer 1:** "statistics upgrade … the cheapest way to shrink the penalty class" is loose. The 256.57-bit term 6log₂(21/ε_s)+log₂(2/ε_c) is the PA/chain-rule/verification cost and is *independent* of the concentration inequalities; Kato/Mannalath shrink the fluctuation widths inside s_X,0, s_X,1, and γ (and possibly the number of union-bound events), not this fixed term. The numbers are per-declared-step fractional changes (CFR N-12), and §11.2's caveat about incomparable step units partially covers this, but "slope" invites misreading. 5. **λ_EC construction:** the "binomial-quantile (logarithm-of-coefficient)" expression is never written down. Its 4.7% overhead over n_X·h₂(QBER) is consistent with the description, but the exact closed form (quantile level, and where its failure probability is charged — ε_c via the verification hash, or the 21?) should be printed for line-by-line audit. 7. **§6 formatting:** the "Reproduced work" and "Newly analyzed work" paragraphs are run together ("…any physical system.**Newly analyzed work…") — missing paragraph break.
8. **§9.3 faithfulness sentence:** "the frozen fixture satisfies this internally through β=ln(21/ε_s)" is a model-inspection finding on artifact 01, which is EV-1c/EV-4 (not byte-hash-verified). Consider tagging that clause with its evidence class, as is done elsewhere.

## 2. Major comments

**M1. The "two open proof problems" claim misstates the controlled register (D5 §6) and silently drops the certification-composability caveat.** Manuscript locations: §6 ("two genuine open proof problems"), §12 "Open proof problems" ("Two gaps are genuine literature gaps"), §16 item 7, §17, and claim C25. Evidence: D5 §6 enumerates **four** genuine open items — (1) detector-side correlated afterpulsing in finite-key decoy proofs, (2) rate-dependent yields inside the decoy method, (3) **full composable integration of certification** (Tan–Nahar Appendix C; D6 §4.3 records that "Tan–Nahar Appendix C notes full composable integration has open technical aspects" and that Q-Orbit adopts the conservative union bound *because of it*), and (4) complete correlated-memory detector treatment. The manuscript carries item (1) and (2) as its fixed two-item list, represents (4) only obliquely inside Table 7 row 13 ("complete treatment open"), and drops (3) **entirely** — precisely in the sections (§9.4, §14.3) where it presents the additive union bound ε_total = ε_c + ε_s + ε_char (+ ε_auth) and Eq. (6) as "the only valid end-to-end security statement." Why it matters: in a manuscript whose core contribution is fail-closed fidelity to a controlled record, a claim-ledger entry (C25, "LITERATURE-SUPPORTED (gap finding)") that hard-codes a two-item gap register against a four-item source register is exactly the defect class the package exists to catch; and omitting the composability caveat where the union bound is adopted slightly overstates how settled the Layer-3 composition step is. This is an omission of a documented caveat, not an over-claim of coverage, so it is correctable with text.

**M2. §11.3 contains a numerical claim absent from the controlled record.** Manuscript location: §11.3 Interpretation — "21 of 41 extraneous-count columns contain no positive cell." Evidence: this figure appears in neither CFR v1.2 §2 (N-01…N-27) nor the claim ledger (CL-01…CL-65); the CFR's stated purpose is that downstream documents "must draw numbers ONLY from this record, with the stated evidence class," and the ledger's source policy repeats this. The adjacent cap-binding count (1,088/1,681) is properly ledger-registered (CL-05, EV-1b), so the omission is specific to this one number. Why it matters: the package's anti-fabrication architecture treats unregistered numbers as Category-2 risks in its own guard specification (D6 §6); an unregistered screen-derived statistic in the Results section is a traceability hole in the manuscript's flagship discipline, even though it is almost certainly recomputable from artifact 05 (EV-1b).

## 2. Major comments

1. **§11.2, line 296 — false quantitative claim.** "a 0.1 dB step moves it by over 8% of baseline, **an order of magnitude more than any other tested parameter per unit step**." The paper's own Table 4 gives rank-1 = −0.08216 and rank-2 = +0.03567 per declared step: ratio **2.30×**, not ~10× (likewise in bits/step: 3,396 vs 1,475). *Why it matters:* a numerical mischaracterization in the Results section of a paper whose central claim is numerical exactitude; it would also mislead a reader about how dominant the loss sensitivity actually is. *Fix:* "roughly 2.3 times the next-largest response (detector efficiency)".

2. **Reference entries 39 and 42 are the same work, listed twice with inconsistent author lists, and double-cited in-text.** Entry 39: Nahar, Tupkary, Lütkenhaus, Quantum **10, 2044** (2026). Entry 42: "S. Nahar, N. Lütkenhaus," arXiv:2503.06328 (2025) PREPRINT. Externally verified: Quantum 10, 2044's eprint is exactly arXiv:2503.06328 (quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2026-03-24-2044); arXiv lists three authors including Tupkary. Entry 42 therefore (a) duplicates entry 39, (b) omits an author, (c) carries a PREPRINT label although the journal record is confirmed two entries away — violating the manuscript's own §19 two-source rule and D3's preprint-labeling rule. (2026) … and Nahar and Lütkenhaus (2025, preprint)"). *Why it matters:* the manuscript's headline contribution is fail-closed citation hygiene; a duplicated, mis-authored record in the reference list is precisely the defect class the package claims to exclude. *Fix:* delete entry 42, keep the published record, and collapse the §7.6 sentence to a single citation (or, if the authors insist the preprint record is distinct, provide the distinguishing evidence — I found none).

3. **Change log vs. manuscript contradiction on caption vocabulary.** Change log §4 item V-5.3 records "non-sanctioned caption token 'documentation' — FIXED to the closed evidence-class vocabulary." But manuscript Figure 4 (line 368) and Figure 5 (line 386) captions still read "Content class: theoretical/documentation", and "documentation" is not in QO-FIGSPEC-001 §0's closed set {modeled, deterministic, theoretical, measured, not measured}. The captions do contain "theoretical" and a not-measured disclaimer, so the evidentiary intent is met; the defect is the stale fix record plus the vocabulary violation. *Fix:* change both captions to "theoretical — …; not measured" (or amend the figspec vocabulary and the change log).
